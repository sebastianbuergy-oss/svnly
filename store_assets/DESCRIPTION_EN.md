# Description – English

Every day, the whole world gets the same challenge. You have seven seconds and one take to capture a real moment.

How SVNLY works:

- Open the daily challenge.
- Record directly in the app—no gallery import and no editing.
- Recording stops automatically after seven seconds.
- The daily feed unlocks only after your take is submitted.
- Discover friends, your country and the world.
- React, comment, follow, report or block with clear safety controls.
- Build your streak and follow your ranking.

One take means no voluntary retakes. A retry is only offered after a verified technical failure.

7 seconds. One take. Be real.
